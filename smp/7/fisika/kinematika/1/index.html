Lembar Teori & Rumus Cepat Modul

1. Kelajuan, Papasan & Menyusul

Segitiga Rumus: $s = v \times t \iff v = \dfrac{s}{t} \iff t = \dfrac{s}{v}$

Konversi: $\text{km/jam} \xrightarrow{\div 3{,}6} \text{m/s}$ dan $\text{m/s} \xrightarrow{\times 3{,}6} \text{km/jam}$.

Kelajuan Rata-Rata: $\bar{v} = \dfrac{s_{\text{total}}}{t_{\text{total}}}$

Papasan Berhadapan: $t = \dfrac{s}{v_1 + v_2}$ | Menyusul: $t = \dfrac{\Delta s}{v_{\text{cepat}} - v_{\text{lambat}}}$

Kereta Melintasi Jembatan: $s_{\text{total}} = L_{\text{kereta}} + L_{\text{jembatan}}$

2. Percepatan ($a$) & GLBB

Rumus Pokok: $a = \dfrac{v_t - v_0}{t} \iff v_t = v_0 + (a \times t) \iff t = \dfrac{v_t - v_0}{a}$

Mula-mula diam: $v_0 = 0$ | Direm hingga berhenti: $v_t = 0$

Gerak Vertikal ke Atas: Diperlambat oleh gravitasi $g$, saat di puncak $v_t = 0 \Rightarrow t_{\text{puncak}} = \dfrac{v_0}{g}$.

3. Resultan Gaya ($\Sigma F$) & Berat Planet

Searah: $\Sigma F = F_1 + F_2$ | Berlawanan: $\Sigma F = F_{\text{besar}} - F_{\text{kecil}}$

Kesetimbangan: $\Sigma F = 0 \iff F_{\text{kiri}} = F_{\text{kanan}}$

Massa ($m$): Tetap di mana pun di alam semesta (kg).

Berat ($w$): $w = m \times g$ (Newton).

4. Dinamika Newton (Hukum II Newton)

Hukum II Newton: $\Sigma F = m \times a \iff a = \dfrac{\Sigma F}{m} \iff m = \dfrac{\Sigma F}{a}$

Ada Gesekan ($f_g$): $\Sigma F = F_{\text{dorong}} - f_g \Rightarrow a = \dfrac{F_{\text{dorong}} - f_g}{m}$

Benda Gandeng Berdampingan: $a = \dfrac{F}{m_1 + m_2}$
